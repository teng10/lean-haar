1.3 Double Commutant Theorem and Schur-Weyl Duality

Using representation theory, we can establish the Double Commutant Theorem for the permutation algebra.

Definition 1.3.1 Permutation Monoid Homomorphism

SchurWeyl.permMonoidHomlem:permAction_tprod, def:permAction, def:TensVThe permutation action as a monoid homomorphism \(\rho _{\text{mon}}: S_k \to \text{End}(V^{\otimes k})\) mapping \(\sigma \mapsto W_\sigma \).

Definition 1.3.2 Permutation Representation

SchurWeyl.permRepdef:permMonoidHom, def:TensVThe permutation representation is the representation \(\rho : S_k \to \text{End}(V^{\otimes k})\) obtained from the permutation monoid homomorphism.

Definition 1.3.3 Permutation Module

SchurWeyl.PermModuledef:permRep, def:TensVThe space \(V^{\otimes k}\) viewed as a \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\)-module under the permutation representation.

Definition 1.3.4 Permutation Algebra Homomorphism

SchurWeyl.permAlgHomdef:permRep, def:TensVThe algebra homomorphism \(\tilde{\rho }: \mathbb {C}[S_k] \to \text{End}(V^{\otimes k})\) induced by the representation.

Lemma 1.3.5 Evaluation of Permutation Algebra Homomorphism

SchurWeyl.permAlgHom_ofdef:permAlgHom, def:permAction, def:permRepThe permutation algebra homomorphism evaluated on a permutation \(\sigma \in S_k\) is the permutation operator \(W_\sigma \):

\begin{equation} \tilde{\rho }(\sigma ) = W_\sigma \end{equation}

Definition 1.3.6 Centralizer to EndModule Mapping

SchurWeyl.centralizer_to_endModuledef:PermModule, def:permRep, def:permImage, thm:permAlgHom_range_eq, def:TensVThe mapping that identifies any \(\mathbb {C}\)-linear endomorphism \(f\) in the centralizer of the span of permutation operators as a \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\)-linear endomorphism of the permutation module.

Definition 1.3.7 Double Centralizer to EndEndModule Mapping

SchurWeyl.doubleCentralizer_to_endEndModuledef:PermModule, def:permImage, lem:endModule_mem_centralizer, def:TensVThe mapping that identifies any \(\mathbb {C}\)-linear endomorphism \(T\) in the double centralizer of the span of permutation operators as an endomorphism over the \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\)-linear endomorphism ring of the permutation module.

Lemma 1.3.8 EndModule is in Centralizer

SchurWeyl.endModule_mem_centralizerdef:PermModule, def:permImage, def:permRep, def:TensVAny \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\)-linear endomorphism of the permutation module restricts to a \(\mathbb {C}\)-linear endomorphism that lies in the centralizer of the span of permutation operators.

Theorem 1.3.9 Range of Algebra Homomorphism

SchurWeyl.permAlgHom_range_eqdef:permAlgHom, def:permImage, lem:permAlgHom_ofThe image of the group algebra homomorphism is the span of the permutation operators:

\begin{equation} \text{Im}(\tilde{\rho }) = \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \end{equation}

Lemma 1.3.10 Membership in Span of Permutation Operators

SchurWeyl.mem_span_permImage_of_mem_rangedef:permAlgHom, def:permImage, thm:permAlgHom_range_eqAny operator \(f \in \text{End}(V^{\otimes k})\) that lies in the range of the permutation algebra homomorphism belongs to the span of the permutation operators:

\begin{equation} f \in \text{Im}(\tilde{\rho }) \implies f \in \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \end{equation}

Lemma 1.3.11 Semisimplicity of Permutation Module

SchurWeyl.permModule_isSemisimpledef:permRep, def:PermModule, def:TensVThe permutation module \(V^{\otimes k}\) is a semisimple \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\)-module. This is a consequence of Maschke’s theorem.

Lemma 1.3.12 Finiteness of Permutation Module over Endomorphism Ring

SchurWeyl.permModule_finite_over_endRingdef:PermModule, def:TensVThe permutation module \(V^{\otimes k}\) is finitely generated as a module over its \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\)-endomorphism ring.

Theorem 1.3.13 Jacobson Density for Permutation Representation

SchurWeyl.permModule_toModuleEnd_surjectivelem:permModule_isSemisimple, lem:permModule_finite_over_endRing, def:permRep, def:TensVThe natural map from the group algebra \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\) to the double endomorphism ring of the permutation module is surjective.

Theorem 1.3.14 Double Commutant containment for Permutation Algebra

SchurWeyl.double_centralizer_permImage’thm:permModule_toModuleEnd_surjective, def:permImage, def:doubleCentralizer_to_endEndModule, lem:mem_span_permImage_of_mem_range, def:permAlgHomThe double centralizer of the span of permutation operators is contained in the span itself:

\begin{equation} \text{Comm}(\text{Comm}(\text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}))) \subseteq \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \end{equation}

Theorem 1.3.15 Double Commutant for Permutation Algebra

SchurWeyl.double_centralizer_permImagedef:permImage, thm:double_centralizer_permImage’The double centralizer of the span of permutation operators is the span itself:

\begin{equation} \text{Comm}(\text{Comm}(\text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}))) = \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \end{equation}
