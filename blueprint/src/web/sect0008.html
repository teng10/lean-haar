2.3 The Haar Moment Operator

We define the normalized Haar measure on the unitary group and use it to define the moment operator.

Theorem 2.3.1 Unitary Entry Norm Bound

SchurWeyl.unitary_entry_norm_le_oneEach entry of a unitary matrix \(M \in U(d)\) has norm at most \(1\).

Theorem 2.3.2 Compactness of the Unitary Group

SchurWeyl.isCompact_unitaryGroupthm:unitary_entry_norm_le_oneThe unitary group \(U(d)\) is a compact subset of the space of \(d \times d\) complex matrices.

Theorem 2.3.3 Compact Space Instance of Unitary Group

SchurWeyl.instCompactSpaceUnitaryGroupthm:isCompact_unitaryGroupThe unitary group \(U(d)\) is a compact topological space.

Theorem 2.3.4 Second Countability of Matrix Space

SchurWeyl.instSecondCountableMatrixThe space of \(d \times d\) complex matrices has second countable topology.

Theorem 2.3.5 Measurable Space Instance of Unitary Group

SchurWeyl.instMeasurableSpaceUnitaryGroupThe unitary group \(U(d)\) carries the Borel \(\sigma \)-algebra.

Theorem 2.3.6 Borel Space Instance of Unitary Group

SchurWeyl.instBorelSpaceUnitaryGroupThe unitary group \(U(d)\) is a Borel space.

Theorem 2.3.7 Haar Measure of Universe is Positive

SchurWeyl.haar_univ_ne_zeroThe left Haar measure of the entire unitary group is positive.

Theorem 2.3.8 Haar Measure of Universe is Finite

SchurWeyl.haar_univ_ne_topThe left Haar measure of the compact unitary group is finite.

Definition 2.3.9 Haar Probability Measure

SchurWeyl.haarProbThe Haar probability measure \(\mu _H\) is the left Haar measure on the compact unitary group \(U(d)\) normalized to total mass 1.

Theorem 2.3.10 Haar Probability is Probability Measure

SchurWeyl.instIsProbabilityMeasureHaarProbdef:haarProbThe Haar probability measure \(\mu _H\) is indeed a probability measure (has total mass 1).

Theorem 2.3.11 Haar Probability is Left Invariant

SchurWeyl.instIsMulLeftInvariantHaarProbdef:haarProbThe Haar probability measure \(\mu _H\) is left-invariant under multiplication.

Definition 2.3.12 Endomorphism of Matrix

SchurWeyl.endOfThe representation of a matrix \(U \in \text{Mat}_{d \times d}(\mathbb {C})\) as a linear endomorphism of \(\mathbb {C}^d\).

Theorem 2.3.13 Multiplicativity of endOf

SchurWeyl.endOf_muldef:endOfThe map ‘endOf‘ satisfies \(\text{endOf}(A B) = \text{endOf}(A) \circ \text{endOf}(B)\).

Theorem 2.3.14 Identity Matrix endOf

SchurWeyl.endOf_onedef:endOfThe map ‘endOf‘ maps the identity matrix to the identity endomorphism.

Theorem 2.3.15 Diagonal Action Composition

SchurWeyl.diagAction_compdef:diagActionThe diagonal action is multiplicative: \(g^{\otimes k} \circ h^{\otimes k} = (g \circ h)^{\otimes k}\).

Theorem 2.3.16 Diagonal Action Identity

SchurWeyl.diagAction_iddef:diagActionThe diagonal action of the identity endomorphism is the identity on the tensor space.

Definition 2.3.17 Conjugation Action

SchurWeyl.actOndef:diagAction, def:endOfFor \(O \in \text{End}(V^{\otimes k})\) and \(U \in U(d)\), the conjugation action is:

\begin{equation} U^{\otimes k} O (U^\dagger )^{\otimes k} \end{equation}
5

Theorem 2.3.18 Unitary Diagonal Action Left Inverse

SchurWeyl.diagAction_endOf_unitary_leftdef:endOf, def:diagAction, thm:diagAction_comp, thm:endOf_mul, thm:endOf_one, thm:diagAction_idFor any unitary matrix \(U\), \((U^\dagger )^{\otimes k} \circ U^{\otimes k} = \text{id}\).

Theorem 2.3.19 Unitary Diagonal Action Right Inverse

SchurWeyl.diagAction_endOf_unitary_rightdef:endOf, def:diagAction, thm:diagAction_comp, thm:endOf_mul, thm:endOf_one, thm:diagAction_idFor any unitary matrix \(U\), \(U^{\otimes k} \circ (U^\dagger )^{\otimes k} = \text{id}\).

Theorem 2.3.20 Continuity of Conjugation Action Entries

SchurWeyl.continuous_actOn_entrydef:actOn, def:toEndMatrix, def:diagAction, lem:toEndMatrix_diagActionFor any operator \(O \in \text{End}(V^{\otimes k})\), the matrix entries of the conjugation action \(\text{actOn}(O, U)\) are continuous functions of \(U \in U(d)\).

Theorem 2.3.21 Integrability of Conjugation Action Entries

SchurWeyl.integrable_actOn_entrythm:continuous_actOn_entry, thm:instCompactSpaceUnitaryGroup, def:haarProb, def:toEndMatrix, def:actOnEach matrix entry of the conjugation action is integrable with respect to the Haar probability measure.

Definition 2.3.22 Haar Moment Matrix

SchurWeyl.momentMatrixdef:actOn, def:toEndMatrix, def:haarProbThe matrix formed by integrating the entries of the conjugation action over the unitary group.

Definition 2.3.23 Haar Moment Operator

SchurWeyl.momentOpdef:momentMatrix, def:toEndMatrixThe Haar moment operator \(\mathbb {E}_{U \sim \mu _H}[U^{\otimes k} O (U^\dagger )^{\otimes k}]\), defined as the operator whose matrix in the computational basis is the Haar average of the conjugation action.

Theorem 2.3.24 Matrix of Haar Moment Operator

SchurWeyl.toEndMatrix_momentOpdef:momentOp, def:momentMatrix, def:toEndMatrixThe matrix representation of the Haar moment operator matches the Haar moment matrix.

Theorem 2.3.25 Pointwise Trace Invariance under Conjugation

SchurWeyl.trace_permDual_actOndef:permDual, def:actOn, def:diagAction, def:endOf,thm:diagAction_endOf_unitary_left, thm:permAction_diagAction_commThe trace against \(V_d^\dagger (\sigma )\) is invariant under unitary conjugation of the operator.

Theorem 2.3.26 Trace Invariance of Moment Operator

SchurWeyl.momentOp_tracedef:momentOp, def:permDual, def:toEndMatrix, def:actOn, thm:trace_permDual_actOn, thm:toEndMatrix_momentOp, thm:integrable_actOn_entry, def:haarProbThe moment operator preserves the traces:

\begin{equation} \text{Tr}(V_d^\dagger (\sigma ) \mathbb {E}[U^{\otimes k} O (U^\dagger )^{\otimes k}]) = \text{Tr}(V_d^\dagger (\sigma ) O) \end{equation}
6

Theorem 2.3.27 Translation of Conjugation Action

SchurWeyl.conj_actOndef:diagAction, def:endOf, def:actOn, thm:diagAction_compConjugating the action by a unitary matrix \(W\) corresponds to left translating the variable in the conjugation action: \(W^{\otimes k} (\text{actOn}(O, U)) (W^\dagger )^{\otimes k} = \text{actOn}(O, W U)\).

Theorem 2.3.28 Unitary Invariance of Haar Moment Operator

SchurWeyl.momentOp_conj_unitarydef:actOn, def:endOf, thm:diagAction_comp, def:diagAction, thm:endOf_mul, def:momentOp, def:momentMatrix, thm:toEndMatrix_momentOp, def:toEndMatrix, def:tensorBasis, def:haarProb, thm:integrable_actOn_entry, thm:conj_actOnThe Haar moment operator is invariant under conjugation by any unitary matrix.

Theorem 2.3.29 Unitary Commutativity of Moment Operator

SchurWeyl.momentOp_comm_unitarydef:momentOp, def:diagAction, def:endOf, thm:momentOp_conj_unitary, thm:diagAction_endOf_unitary_leftThe moment operator commutes with \(W^{\otimes k}\) for any unitary matrix \(W \in U(d)\).

We extend this to all matrices using density.

Theorem 2.3.30 Orthonormal Basis from Matrix Columns

SchurWeyl.exists_orthonormalBasis_colsAn orthonormal family can be constructed from the columns of a matrix \(N\) when normalized.

Theorem 2.3.31 Spectral Decomposition of Hermetian Matrix

SchurWeyl.svd_spectralAny positive semidefinite Hermetian matrix \(M^\dagger M\) can be diagonalized by a unitary matrix.

Theorem 2.3.32 Conjugate Transpose Multiplicative Spectral Relation

SchurWeyl.conjTranspose_mul_self_of_spectralthm:svd_spectralIf \(M^\dagger M = Q D Q^\dagger \), then \((M Q)^\dagger (M Q) = D\).

Theorem 2.3.33 Unitary Matrix from Orthonormal Basis

SchurWeyl.exists_unitary_of_orthonormalBasisAn orthonormal basis can be assembled into a unitary matrix.

Theorem 2.3.34 Column Normalization Relation

SchurWeyl.cols_mul_diag_sqrtThe unitary matrix times the diagonal square root matrix recovers the normalized columns.

Theorem 2.3.35 SVD Existence

SchurWeyl.svd_existsthm:svd_spectral, thm:conjTranspose_mul_self_of_spectral, thm:exists_orthonormalBasis_cols, thm:exists_unitary_of_orthonormalBasis, thm:cols_mul_diag_sqrtAny complex matrix can be written as \(M = U_1 D U_2\) with \(U_1, U_2 \in U(d)\) and diagonal \(D\).

Theorem 2.3.36 Matrix of Diagonal Diagonal Action

SchurWeyl.toEndMatrix_diagAction_diagonaldef:toEndMatrix, def:diagAction, def:endOf, lem:diagAction_diagonalThe matrix representation of the diagonal action of a diagonal matrix is diagonal in the tensor basis.

Theorem 2.3.37 Diagonal Matrix Unitary Criterion

SchurWeyl.diagonal_mem_unitaryGroupA diagonal matrix with entries on the unit circle is unitary.

Theorem 2.3.38 Identity extension from Torus

SchurWeyl.prod_eq_of_torusAn identity of index-monomials holding on the unit torus holds on all inputs.

Theorem 2.3.39 Matrix of Composition

SchurWeyl.toEndMatrix_compdef:toEndMatrix, def:tensorBasisThe matrix representation map is multiplicative: \(\text{toEndMatrix}(f \circ g) = \text{toEndMatrix}(f) \text{toEndMatrix}(g)\).

Theorem 2.3.40 Entrywise Commutation with Diagonal Action

SchurWeyl.comm_diagAction_diagonal_entrydef:diagAction, def:endOf, thm:toEndMatrix_comp, thm:toEndMatrix_diagAction_diagonal, thm:diagonal_mem_unitaryGroup, thm:prod_eq_of_torusAn operator commuting with all unitary diagonal actions commutes entrywise with all diagonal actions.

Theorem 2.3.41 Commutation with all Diagonal Actions

SchurWeyl.comm_diagAction_diagonaldef:diagAction, def:endOf, def:toEndMatrix, thm:comm_diagAction_diagonal_entry, thm:toEndMatrix_comp, thm:toEndMatrix_diagAction_diagonalAn operator commuting with all unitary diagonal actions commutes with the diagonal action of any diagonal matrix.

Theorem 2.3.42 Unitary Commutant implies Centralizer

SchurWeyl.mem_centralizer_of_comm_unitarydef:diagAction, thm:svd_exists, thm:comm_diagAction_diagonal, def:endOf, thm:endOf_mul, thm:diagAction_compAn operator that commutes with all unitary diagonal actions commutes with all diagonal actions.

Theorem 2.3.43 Moment Operator lies in Permutation Span

SchurWeyl.momentOp_mem_spandef:momentOp, thm:momentOp_comm_unitary, thm:mem_centralizer_of_comm_unitary, thm:schur_weylThe Haar moment operator lies in the span of the permutation operators:
