1.3 Double Commutant Theorem and Schur-Weyl Duality

Using representation theory, we can establish the Double Commutant Theorem for the permutation algebra.

Definition 1.3.1 Permutation Representation

SchurWeyl.permRepdef:permActionThe permutation representation is the monoid homomorphism \(\rho : S_k \to \text{End}(V^{\otimes k})\) mapping \(\sigma \mapsto W_\sigma \).

Definition 1.3.2 Permutation Module

SchurWeyl.PermModuledef:permRepThe space \(V^{\otimes k}\) viewed as a \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\)-module under the permutation representation.

Definition 1.3.3 Permutation Algebra Homomorphism

SchurWeyl.permAlgHomdef:permRepThe algebra homomorphism \(\tilde{\rho }: \mathbb {C}[S_k] \to \text{End}(V^{\otimes k})\) induced by the representation.

Theorem 1.3.4 Range of Algebra Homomorphism

SchurWeyl.permAlgHom_range_eqdef:permAlgHom, def:permImageThe image of the group algebra homomorphism is the span of the permutation operators:

\begin{equation} \text{Im}(\tilde{\rho }) = \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \end{equation}

Lemma 1.3.5 Semisimplicity of Permutation Module

SchurWeyl.permModule_isSemisimpledef:permRepThe permutation module \(V^{\otimes k}\) is a semisimple \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\)-module. This is a consequence of Maschke’s theorem.

Theorem 1.3.6 Jacobson Density for Permutation Representation

SchurWeyl.permModule_toModuleEnd_surjectivelem:permModule_isSemisimpleThe natural map from the group algebra \(\mathbb {C}[S_k]\) to the double endomorphism ring of the permutation module is surjective.

Theorem 1.3.7 Double Commutant for Permutation Algebra

SchurWeyl.double_centralizer_permImagethm:permModule_toModuleEnd_surjective, thm:permAlgHom_range_eq, def:permImageThe double centralizer of the span of permutation operators is the span itself:

\begin{equation} \text{Comm}(\text{Comm}(\text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}))) = \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \end{equation}

We now assemble the main result.

Theorem 1.3.8 Hard Direction of Schur-Weyl Duality

SchurWeyl.centralizer_diagImage_le_span_permImagethm:centralizer_permImage_le_span_diagImage, thm:double_centralizer_permImage, def:diagImage, def:permImageThe centralizer of the diagonal image is contained in the span of the permutation image:

\begin{equation} \text{Comm}(\mathcal{D}_{d,k}) \subseteq \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \end{equation}

Theorem 1.3.9 Easy Direction of Schur-Weyl Duality

SchurWeyl.span_permImage_le_centralizer_diagImagethm:permImage_subset_centralizer_diagImageThe span of the permutation image is contained in the centralizer of the diagonal image:

\begin{equation} \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \subseteq \text{Comm}(\mathcal{D}_{d,k}) \end{equation}

Theorem 1.3.10 Schur-Weyl Duality

SchurWeyl.schur_weylthm:centralizer_diagImage_le_span_permImage, thm:span_permImage_le_centralizer_diagImage, def:diagImage, def:permImageThe centralizer of the diagonal image is exactly equal to the span of the permutation image:

\begin{equation} \text{Comm}(\mathcal{D}_{d,k}) = \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \end{equation}
