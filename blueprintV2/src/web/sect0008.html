2.3 The Haar Moment Operator

We define the normalized Haar measure on the unitary group and use it to define the moment operator.

Definition 2.3.1 Haar Probability Measure

SchurWeyl.haarProbThe Haar probability measure \(\mu _H\) is the left Haar measure on the compact unitary group \(U(d)\) normalized to total mass 1.

Definition 2.3.2 Conjugation Action

SchurWeyl.actOndef:diagActionFor \(O \in \text{End}(V^{\otimes k})\) and \(U \in U(d)\), the conjugation action is:

\begin{equation} U^{\otimes k} O (U^\dagger )^{\otimes k} \end{equation}
5

Definition 2.3.3 Haar Moment Operator

SchurWeyl.momentOpdef:actOnThe Haar moment operator \(\mathbb {E}_{U \sim \mu _H}[U^{\otimes k} O (U^\dagger )^{\otimes k}]\) is the entrywise average of the conjugation action.

Theorem 2.3.4 Trace Invariance of Moment Operator

SchurWeyl.momentOp_tracedef:momentOp, def:permDualThe moment operator preserves the traces:

\begin{equation} \text{Tr}(V_d^\dagger (\sigma ) \mathbb {E}[U^{\otimes k} O (U^\dagger )^{\otimes k}]) = \text{Tr}(V_d^\dagger (\sigma ) O) \end{equation}
6

Theorem 2.3.5 Unitary Commutativity of Moment Operator

SchurWeyl.momentOp_comm_unitarydef:momentOpThe moment operator commutes with \(W^{\otimes k}\) for any unitary matrix \(W \in U(d)\).

We extend this to all matrices using density.

Theorem 2.3.6 SVD Existence

SchurWeyl.svd_existsAny complex matrix can be written as \(M = U_1 D U_2\) with \(U_1, U_2 \in U(d)\) and diagonal \(D\).

Theorem 2.3.7 Unitary Commutant implies Centralizer

SchurWeyl.mem_centralizer_of_comm_unitarythm:svd_existsAn operator that commutes with all unitary diagonal actions commutes with all diagonal actions.

Theorem 2.3.8 Moment Operator lies in Permutation Span

SchurWeyl.momentOp_mem_spandef:momentOp, thm:momentOp_comm_unitary, thm:mem_centralizer_of_comm_unitary, thm:schur_weylThe Haar moment operator lies in the span of the permutation operators:

\begin{equation} \mathbb {E}_{U}[U^{\otimes k} O (U^\dagger )^{\otimes k}] \in \text{Span}(\mathcal{P}_{d,k}) \end{equation}
7

Theorem 2.3.9 Weingarten Moments for Haar Integral

SchurWeyl.weingarten_moment_haardef:momentOp, thm:momentOp_mem_span, thm:momentOp_trace, thm:weingarten_moment_decomposition_of_propsThe Haar moment operator is a linear combination of the permutation operators whose coefficients solve the Weingarten system.
